11 Interaction Archetype Families (Part 3)

Canonical patterns covering all forms of interaction in CRLD content:

  • Direct Manipulation: Drag, slide, rotate, resize representations
  • Reveal & Conceal: Hide/show, toggles, progressive disclosure
  • Constraint & Liberation: Toggle constraints on, observe effects
  • Prediction & Feedback: Make prediction, reveal truth, compare
  • Parameter Sweep: Vary one parameter, observe dependent variable
  • Comparative Tables: Side-by-side comparison of cases
  • Decomposition: Break complex form into parts, recombine
  • Sequencing & Ordering: Order entities by property, verify
  • Construction: Build or draw a mathematical object
  • Pattern Completion: Fill gaps in sequence or pattern
  • Classification: Sort items into categories with feedback

19 Cognitive Actions (Part 5)

Mental operations induced by interactions, including: Compare, Predict, Classify, Infer cause, Generalize, Measure, Test invariance, Estimate, Verify, Optimize, Decompose, Translate, Interpret, Analyze, Synthesize, Hypothesize, Recall, Recognize, Apply.

Core Principle (§0): ClassRoots philosophy operationalizes through rulebooks (Layer 01), engines (Layer 02), and agents (Layer 04). All design decisions trace back to these foundational documents.
