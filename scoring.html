Score Bands & Verdicts

Ship-Ready ≥85%
Ready for publication. All Tier 1 categories ≥70% of their weight.
Upgradeable 70–84%
Targeted fixes, no rewrite needed. Human may choose to ship or apply fixes.
Re-Architect 50–69%
Structural rethink required. Major redesign needed. Triggers P9 Remediation Loop.
Reframe <50%
Fundamental LO misalignment or scope failure. Triggers P10 Reframe Loop.

Partition Rules (NEW)

M-4.x Partition Rule
M-4.1–M-4.3 (systematic variation, non-examples, counter-examples) → scored under Category V (Rev-Ped)
M-4.4–M-4.5 (local context relevance, curriculum-aligned examples) → scored under Category J (Rev-Loc)

This partition resolves prior dual-assignment and enforces Exclusive Role Ownership (R4).

T1-M1 Partition Rule
Category A: Scores T1-M1 for structural prerequisite coverage — whether assumed knowledge is explicitly taught or declared as prerequisite
Category C: Scores T1-M1 for sequence flow continuity — whether progression flows logically without conceptual gaps

A single T1-M1 violation is scored in ONE category only: Category A if missing prerequisites, Category C if broken flow.

Upgrade Map Alternatives (NEW)

When a module scores 70–84% (Upgradeable), review agents may suggest alternatives to address low-scoring categories without full rebuild:

Alternative-Eligible Categories: Tropes, Misconceptions, Digital_Delight, Interaction_Alternatives

Max 3 alternatives per operation (configurable via {{CONFIG: upgrade_map.alternatives_enabled}})

Upgrade Map Alternatives are logged in REVIEW_REPORT § defects, validated against RESEARCH_BRIEF, and made available to Build Orchestrator for Mode C (rebuild with targeted fixes).

Research Alignment Checks (RA-1 through RA-8)

RA-1: LO Consistency

LO in module PPTX matches LO_BRIEF derived by research phase

RA-2: CPA Progression Match

Module follows CPA progression (Concrete → Pictorial → Abstract) as per RESEARCH_BRIEF

RA-3: Prerequisite Validation

All prerequisites listed in RESEARCH_BRIEF are activated or assumed in module

RA-4: Visual Model Alignment

Visual models match RESEARCH_BRIEF § visual recommendations (no extraneous diagrams)

RA-5: Interaction Design Integrity

Applets / interactions match RESEARCH_BRIEF § interaction landscape analysis

RA-6: Difficulty Progression

Module difficulty matches gradient in RESEARCH_BRIEF (no surprises, no boring stretches)

RA-7: Misconception Addressing

Common misconceptions from RESEARCH_BRIEF are explicitly addressed or prevented

RA-8: Localization / Context Fit

Examples, cultural references, and curricula alignment match localization context in RESEARCH_BRIEF

Configuration: Score band thresholds (Ship-ready ≥85%, Upgradeable 70–84%, etc.) are configurable via {{CONFIG: scoring.verdict_*}} parameters in System_Configuration.md. Evolver may propose adjustments based on system-wide patterns.
